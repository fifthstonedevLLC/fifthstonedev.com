Meet the developer

Phil Harberts

Hi, I'm Phil — the founder and developer at Fifth Stone Dev. With a background in both technology and mission-driven work, I bring a unique perspective to every project I build.

I've spent the last few years working with an enterprise-level SaaS product, where I analyzed complex workflows, built internal tools that streamlined operations, and shaped processes within the software support team. That experience taught me to see software from the user's perspective — understanding what makes tools truly useful, where processes break down, and how to build solutions people actually want to use.

Whether it's building a website from scratch, automating tedious processes, developing custom tools, or providing strategic consulting, I'm passionate about creating technology that serves a real purpose. My approach is simple: listen carefully, plan thoroughly, and execute with precision. I believe in building relationships, not just websites.
