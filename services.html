Custom Web Development

We build websites the right way — clean code, fast load times, and a structure designed to scale. Every project starts with understanding your goals, your audience, and what success actually looks like for your organization. You work directly with the developer, not an account manager, so nothing gets lost in translation.

Whether you need a polished marketing site, a content-managed platform, or a full web application with custom functionality, we scope the right solution for your budget and timeline — no over-engineering, no unnecessary complexity.

What's included
  • Responsive, mobile-first design
  • Performance-focused builds
  • SEO-structured from day one
  • CMS & third-party integrations
  • Hosting & ongoing management
02

Business Process Automation

Most businesses are sitting on hundreds of hours of recoverable time — locked in repetitive manual tasks, copy-paste workflows, and disconnected tools that don't talk to each other. We build custom automation solutions that eliminate those bottlenecks and free your team to focus on the work that actually matters.

Our automation engagements start with a discovery phase: we map your current workflows, identify the highest-impact opportunities, and build targeted solutions. Clients have seen time savings exceeding 90% on previously manual processes.

What's included
  • Workflow & task automation
  • API & system integration
  • AI-powered automation
  • Custom internal tools
  • Form & communication automation
03

Digital Strategy Consulting

Not every problem requires a full build. Sometimes what you need is an outside perspective — someone who can look at your digital presence objectively, tell you what's actually hurting you, and help you prioritize what to fix first.

We work with small businesses that are managing their own websites, evaluating vendors, or trying to get more out of the tools they already have. Engagements are scoped by the hour or project, with no long-term retainer required.

What's included
  • Website & SEO audits
  • UX/UI analysis
  • Vendor & platform evaluation
  • Digital roadmapping
  • Team training & documentation
